KEY DECISIONS

1. PostgreSQL will remain the primary database and source of truth, with performance testing to be completed before deployment.
2. MongoDB will not be used as primary storage in this project.
3. Migration and rollback will first be tested in the staging environment.
4. Verification of include_internal=false will be added to the deployment checklist.
5. The Wider MR rate will remain external-only.
6. Only Prometheus and Grafana will be evaluated for monitoring for now.
7. At least 500 concurrent requests will be considered the test target for performance testing.
8. Secrets will not be committed to the repository; they have to be kept in environment variables or a secret manager.
9. Staging and production credentials will remain separate.
